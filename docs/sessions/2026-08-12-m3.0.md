## The shape of the session

1. **The harness predates the content — on purpose.** The roadmap's hardest-won lesson is
   that desyncs are correctness emergencies that cannot be retrofitted. The placeholder
   world, hash, and replay CLI all landed before the ECS existed, so every later milestone
   inherits a regression net instead of writing one.
2. **The replay container was version-locked from day one:** magic, version, logic hash,
   seed, tick rate — and the deliberate policy that any format change bumps the logic hash
   and *rejects* old recordings. This is the contract M3.1/M3.2 deliberately broke twice
   (with new hashes) and will break again.
3. **Isolation was proven structurally:** `sim_boundary` scans `engine/sim` sources for
   float/libm/wall-clock/platform/render/Vulkan imports, and `sim_determinism_tests` links
   `eng_sim` directly — no window, no renderer.

---

## Cross-cutting decisions

- **`eng_serialize` was born here** — bounded, sticky, allocation-free little-endian
  readers/writers, the shared codec for replay *and* net (M6.0's "one codec" rule).
- **The hash covers semantic state, not bytes:** fixed-order iteration over live ranges
  only; never pointers, padding, capacity, or storage order. The comparator names the
  first divergent field — that field-diff machinery is the M6.6 divergence monitor's seed.
- **Exit-code taxonomy** (1 usage/I-O, 2 invalid, 3 divergence) made the CLI testable as a
  fixture rather than a human watching stdout.

## Lessons for future sessions

- **A deliberate hash break is a milestone, not a regression.** Each M3.x bump was
  reviewed and recorded; the oracle moves by design.
- **"Reject with a code" beats "fail with a message"** for tooling that must be tested
  headlessly.
- **Deferred (still):** dirty-hash profiling (revisit only if the 30 Hz full-state hash is
  measured slow), Vulkan SDK in CI, clang-cl/UBSan parity run.
